The receipt was read incorrectly

Every field can be edited on the "Check receipt" screen before saving. Flat, well-lit photos read best. For PDF invoices, use Share › Receipt Ring from Mail, Files or Safari.

I'm not getting reminders

Check Settings › Notifications › Receipt Ring on your iPhone. In the app, Settings shows which days and time reminders are sent.

Restoring my Pro purchase

Open Settings in the app and tap "Restore purchases". Use the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your data is stored on your device. Restoring your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup brings it along.
